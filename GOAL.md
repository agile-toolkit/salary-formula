### Salary Formula — Goal

#### Outcome

Become the calculator organisations cite when they move to transparent pay —
and the app most likely to make the suite pay for itself.

#### Why this app can win it

Compensation is the one area in this suite where organisations already spend
money and where a credible free tool changes real decisions. It also carries the
strongest natural path to revenue, because the buyer is an organisation rather
than an individual practitioner.

#### Signals

1. Organisations adopting a formula built here — cited in public pay policies.
2. Willingness-to-pay signals: enquiries about team, private, or white-label use.
3. GitHub stars and inbound links from compensation and HR writing.
4. Scenarios and comparisons saved — depth of use, not just a single glance.

#### Boundaries

- Not payroll; never touches actual payment execution.
- Not an HR record system; no personal employee data.
- Transparency logic stays inspectable — no opaque scoring.
